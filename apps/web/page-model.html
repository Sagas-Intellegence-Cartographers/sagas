Corner shop page model

A gray-box wireframe of one site page, built from the corner shop fixture. It shows what each part of the page holds and which rule applies to it. It does not decide how anything looks. The place, the people and everything they say are invented. Pick a moment, then select a dotted phrase.

1The place

site.name, site.aka, site.address. How well documented the place is, said in words from integrity. Never a number. A thin page reads as early, not as failing.

2What people handed over

records[]. Whether a record is a source or evidence comes from the claims that use it (sourceRecordId, evidenceRecordIds). A record with nothing claimed about it is complete. Uploading is not part of this page.

3Section heading

sections[].heading. Invented for now. What a heading is derived from is open, and it should not change every time a claim arrives.

4The passage

compositions[].text, with spans[] marking the phrases that lead somewhere. When a detail is disputed the passage uses wording both readings fit. It never picks one.

5The claims behind a phrase

spans[].claimIds, and detailIds when the phrase is about one detail. Claims are ordered by weight, which is never shown. An extension sits under the claim it adds to (claim.target.claimId). The detail's excerpt is highlighted in the claim's own words.

6Two readings of one detail

detailStatuses[].competingValues, in the order they arrived, side by side with the same weight, each with who gave it and the reasoning. No winner and no count.

7Leaving a passover

Sounds right, don't know, don't care. Three equal choices with no counts. How to offer them without nudging people toward what others chose is an open design question.

The rules every design keeps

  • No score, count or ranking on screen.
  • Nothing looks settled or official.
  • Disagreement stays visible, side by side.
  • A claim nobody has answered never looks rejected.
  • Every claim leads back to its author and its records.

What this does not decide

Layout, type, color, motion and how deep the page can go are the designer's. This page fixes what information appears and where it comes from.
