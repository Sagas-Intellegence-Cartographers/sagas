# Sagas: provisional terms for contributors

Version `provisional-0.1`. Last updated 25 October 2026. Not reviewed by a
lawyer.

These are provisional terms for a small research pilot. They cover only the
first few contributions. They will be replaced by reviewed terms before Sagas
opens more widely, and you will be asked to agree to the new version.

## Who runs Sagas

Sagas is an open source project run by Civum, Inc. ("we"). It is a map of
community history: people share what they know about places, and others add to
it, question it, or bring evidence.

## Who can contribute

You must be 18 or older to contribute. Anyone may read Sagas.

## What you share stays yours

You keep ownership of anything you contribute: records such as photographs,
recordings, documents, web pages and written stories, and the claims you write
about them.

By contributing, you give the operator of Sagas, and any organization that
takes over running it, permission to:

- store, display and publish your contribution on Sagas, as your choices allow;
- make copies needed to run, back up and move the archive;
- show your contribution alongside other people's claims about the same place;
- let other people write claims about it, and transcribe or translate it, with
  their work credited to them;
- analyze it to connect and order contributions on Sagas.

We will not sell your contributions or use them in other products, and we will
not use them to train AI models (see "How we use AI"). We will only send a contribution to an outside
archive if you ask us to and agree to that archive's own release form.

## Your choices, record by record

Each thing you hand over keeps its own choices, and you can change them at any
time:

- whether the student teams building Sagas may see it and work with it;
- whether it is public now, later (we'll ask you again before it's made
  public), or never;
- for a photo: a photo taken on a phone often records where it was taken. You
  choose whether we keep that, show only how far it is from the place, or
  remove it. If you don't choose, we remove it.

You also tell us whether it mentions other people, living or not, so we can
take care with it.

## How we use AI

Today Sagas doesn't use AI on anything you contribute. We do not use
contributions to train AI models, and we won't.

If we start using AI tools to help translate or transcribe, we'll update this
section before we do, say which outside provider your contribution would be sent
to, and ask you first, record by record. A machine translation or transcript
will always be labelled as one, the original stays the record, and a person can
correct it. We'll only use a provider whose terms don't let it train on what we
send.

## What you confirm

When you contribute, you confirm that:

- it is yours to share, or you have permission from whoever it belongs to;
- if it is a recording or photograph of someone else, that person (or someone
  entitled to speak for them) has agreed to it being shared;
- it is honest to the best of your knowledge.

## What is not allowed

Do not contribute:

- attacks on people because of who they are, including their race, religion,
  ethnicity, sexuality, gender or disability;
- threats, harassment, or private information about another person, such as a
  home address or phone number;
- anything you do not have the right to share.

Historical material that contains offensive language, such as an old newspaper
clipping, may be kept with a note explaining its context.

## Taking something down

You can take your own contribution out of public view at any time by emailing
info@civum.io, and once Sagas lets you change a record's choices yourself, you
can do it there too. You can also ask us to delete it, by emailing the same
address.

Anyone can report a contribution they think breaks these terms, or that uses
their own words, image or recording without permission, by emailing
info@civum.io. We may hide a reported contribution while we look at it.

## What we keep about you

A profile is a way back to your own contributions. It doesn't need your name.
We keep what you contribute and the choices you made for it. We don't track you
across other sites, and we don't sell anything about you.

## No guarantees

Sagas is a pilot. It is provided as it is, may change or stop, and may contain
mistakes. Claims on Sagas are what people have contributed. They are not
statements by Civum, Inc.

## Changes to these terms

We will tell you if these terms change. Each contribution records which version
of the terms was in place when it was made.
